The first in-app protocol used 12 repetitions plus two warmups per session. Four whole pairs completed, but two consecutive following acquisitions could not admit the second side within the required 840-second lease. First-side accepted work took 476.308 and 463.281 seconds, leaving 355.137 and 368.331 seconds. No lease was extended and neither incomplete pair seeds later inference.

This protocol is retained as diagnostic evidence and superseded by a fresh, fixed 20-pair cohort with six repetitions, two warmups and four exposure steps. All recorder code, builds, GPU conditions, action endpoints, state resets, bootstrap and precision thresholds remain fixed. The four completed 12-repeat pairs are not mixed with six-repeat pairs or selectively removed by latency. Raw reports, incomplete acquisitions, configs and the decision are in keep/313b-inapp-timing/r3-budget-superseded.json, aa-inapp-r3-*, and aa-inapp-r3-cont-* on Agentbox and the final ticket NAS snapshot.
